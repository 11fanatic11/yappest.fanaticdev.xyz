Yappest is a free voice changer, soundboard and audio clipper for Windows 10 and 11. One virtual microphone, called Yappest Input, carries your voice, your effects and your sounds.

Download for Windows
Windows 10 or 11 · free · installs for your user account, no admin needed to install

What it does

Voice changerEight voices: Pitch, Sound Barrier, Robot, Demon, Cavern, Radio, Alien and Whisper, each with its own controls.
SoundboardAdd mp3, wav, ogg or mp4 (audio is pulled from the video). Name sounds, give them icons, play from the app or a Stream Deck.
ClippingSave the last 5 to 60 seconds of your mic and your PC's audio with a key. Clips are mp3, transcribed on your PC so you can trim by clicking words.
Stream DeckPlay sounds, clip, mute, and switch voices from your keys.

Good to know

  • The virtual microphone is built on VB-CABLE, a donationware virtual audio device by VB-Audio Software (donations to VB-Audio are welcome). Yappest isn't affiliated with or endorsed by VB-Audio. It's set up for you the first time Yappest runs (Windows asks for permission once).
  • The source is available to read on GitHub under the PolyForm Strict License: you can read and build it for non-commercial use, but not redistribute it.
  • Speech recognition for clips runs entirely on your PC. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Updates are optional. Yappest tells you when a new version is out and only installs if you say yes.
